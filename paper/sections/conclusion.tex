\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under chronological replay, new attack classes and a small, delayed labelling budget, detectors trained once
collapse and simple retraining forgets or floods analysts with reviews. \nova{} combines class-conditional
open-world rejection with recalibration, a novelty-weighted query, conservative label propagation, a
class-balanced memory and a guarded deployment loop. At a 10\% budget it is the best or tied-best method on
open-world macro-F1 on all three datasets, with a lower false-positive rate than the strongest baseline on each; at 1\% it leads on both
CIC datasets. Short-lived attacks that look benign, and the transfer of its accuracy gain to neural classifiers,
remain open problems.
