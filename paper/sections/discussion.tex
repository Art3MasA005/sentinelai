\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{False positives depend on calibration.} Most benign flows flagged by any method are \unk{} decisions, not
attack labels; their rate is set largely by the open-world quantiles (2\% and 99\%), which all methods share. A
2.8\% review rate is still large in absolute terms: at one million benign flows a day it would mean about 28{,}000
reviews (the base-rate fallacy~\cite{arp2022dos}). The quantiles trade review load against recall for every
method alike. We fixed them before the experiments and did not tune them per dataset.

\textbf{Would a learned representation help?} To test whether the hard classes are a representation problem,
we ran an offline probe on CIC-IDS2017 only. At three points in the stream, we trained an embedding on the
labelled pool a 10\%-budget method would have. It was trained either with class-balanced prototypical
episodes~\cite{snell2017prototypical}, or with the same episodes plus one known class held out per episode as a
pseudo-unknown~\cite{liu2020peeler}. We then measured how well the $k$-NN distance to benign flows separates
future unseen attacks. Both embeddings keep the benign false-positive rate close to its 1\% target on later
traffic (0.7--1.4\%, against 3.0--5.6\% for raw features), and they raise the detection rate at that threshold for
DDoS (0.16 to 0.66) and SQL injection (0 to 0.67). They lose Infiltration, however (1.0 to 0), and the open-set
episodes add nothing over plain prototypical training. Bot, PortScan and XSS stay below 5\% detection in every
representation. The remaining errors on these classes therefore reflect overlap with benign traffic in the flow
features, not a lack of capacity, and we did not add the embedding to \nova{}.

\textbf{Dataset errors cap every method.} Some CIC-IDS2017 port-scan flows are feature-identical to benign flows,
and Web Attack -- Brute Force and XSS are barely separable~\cite{engelen2021troubleshooting,lanvin2022errors}.
SSH-Patator and slowloris occur only before any label can arrive under a one-window delay, so no adaptive method
can learn them in time. Several CSE-CIC-IDS2018 day files are truncated at 1{,}048{,}575 rows in the published
CSVs. Results on the corrected CIC-IDS2017~\cite{engelen2021troubleshooting} are future work.

\textbf{Short time spans.} The datasets cover two to ten days, so the stream mixes attack onset with modest benign
drift; long-term benign drift is under-represented.

\textbf{Re-implemented baselines.} Baselines are re-implemented in a common multi-class framework with the same
classifier and labelling cost, not run from the authors' code; what each keeps from its paper is documented in our
repository. NetGuard's generative augmentation is not reproduced.

\textbf{Simulated analyst.} The oracle returns correct labels with a fixed delay; real analysts make mistakes and
answer at variable speed.

\textbf{Hyper-parameters} were frozen after two development runs on CIC-IDS2017 and reused unchanged on the other
datasets.
