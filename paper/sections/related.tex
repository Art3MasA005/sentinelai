\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Evaluation.} TESSERACT~\cite{pendlebury2019tesseract} showed that temporal bias inflates
security-classifier results and introduced the area-under-time (AUT) metric; Arp et al.~\cite{arp2022dos}
catalogue further pitfalls. CIC-IDS2017 and CSE-CIC-IDS2018 contain labelling and feature-extraction
errors~\cite{engelen2021troubleshooting,liu2022error}. We therefore replay data chronologically, fit
preprocessing on the training period only, and run every baseline under one protocol.

\textbf{Drift detection and rejection.} Transcend~\cite{jordaney2017transcend,barbero2022transcending} rejects
low-credibility predictions; CADE~\cite{yang2021cade} and OWAD~\cite{han2023owad} detect and explain drifting
samples. They do not decide what to label or when to deploy an update.

\textbf{Adaptive NIDS.} INSOMNIA~\cite{andresini2021insomnia}, AOC-IDS~\cite{zhang2024aoc} and
SSF~\cite{zhang2025ssf} retrain mostly binary detectors, often from pseudo-labels; replay-based continual
learning~\cite{amalapuram2023augmented} uses class-balanced reservoirs~\cite{chrysakis2020online}.
Fern{\'a}ndez-Barrios et al.~\cite{fernandezbarrios2026candidate} study when a retrained model should replace the
deployed one. Label delay has been studied for stream active learning~\cite{pham2022stream} and malware
pipelines~\cite{botacin2025label}. We are not aware of an adaptive NIDS that rejects unknown traffic per flow
while modelling label delay.

\textbf{Active learning and open-set NIDS.} NetGuard~\cite{gupta2025netguard} queries by a density ratio and
Chen et al.~\cite{chen2023continuous} by contrastive uncertainty, both for a closed class set.
Open-CNN~\cite{zhang2020osr} rejects unknown attacks with extreme-value theory, and Corsini and
Yang~\cite{corsini2023ood} find $k$-NN distance the strongest single out-of-distribution detector on CIC data, in
line with deep $k$-NN in vision~\cite{sun2022knn}. \nova{} combines a class-conditional $k$-NN detector,
recalibrated as classes are learned, with a novelty-weighted query under label delay.
